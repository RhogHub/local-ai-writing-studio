# Generic operation prompt: develop an idea or lesson

Turn the supplied notes, outline, or rough draft into a coherent piece of writing that communicates the central idea clearly.

Identify the main point, organize the supporting ideas in a logical sequence, and explain relationships that are already present in the source. Adapt the structure and tone to the user's intended content type and audience.

Do not force a lesson, moral, conclusion, or instructional format when it does not fit the material. Do not invent facts, experiences, evidence, or recommendations.

Return the complete developed text.
